The WTO Committee on Rules of Origin (CRO) met on 7-8 October to review developments in preferential rules of origin for least-developed countries (LDCs), hear updates from preference-granting members, and exchange experiences on origin administration and trade facilitation. Members also examined notifications on non-preferential rules of origin, discussed digital tools that can improve transparency and compliance with origin requirements, and considered draft annual reports on the Committee's work.

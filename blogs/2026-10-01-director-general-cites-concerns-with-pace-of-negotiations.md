A meeting of the Trade Negotiations Committee (TNC) on 1 October provided WTO members with an opportunity to take stock of the WTO's negotiating agenda six months after the 14th Ministerial Conference (MC14) in Yaoundé and voice their views on how to advance their work. Members heard readouts from the chairs of the negotiating bodies about the state of play in their respective talks.

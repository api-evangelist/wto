At a transparency session on 30 September, facilitators for the discussions on WTO reform reported on recent meetings among members on the four tracks of reform: foundational issues, decision- making, development and level playing field. The facilitators noted a high level of engagement with members' written and oral proposals submitted in recent weeks. They acknowledged that momentum is building across all four tracks, but substantial work remains.

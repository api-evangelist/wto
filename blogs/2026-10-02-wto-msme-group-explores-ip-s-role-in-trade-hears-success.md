The Informal Working Group on Micro, Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (MSMEs) met on 2 October, with a focus on the practical role of intellectual property (IP) in helping small businesses innovate, grow and reach international markets. The meeting included an information session on IP tools and thematic presentations from members, international organizations and stakeholders. Several members reported on MSME initiatives in formal WTO committees.
